About TH-KFRE

    
TH-KFRE is the 4-variable Kidney Failure Risk Equation (Tangri et al., JAMA 2011) recalibrated for Thai patients. It keeps the original coefficients and changes two numbers: the baseline risk and one slope that stretches the risk score. It was developed in 1,057 adults with CKD G3–G5 seen at the nephrology clinic of Faculty of Medicine Vajira Hospital, Bangkok, in 2021–2022 and followed for 2 years (90 kidney failure events). Death before kidney failure was treated as a competing event.

    
Kidney failure means maintenance dialysis for more than 3 months or kidney transplantation. In the study cohort the KFRE ranked patients well (C statistic 0.94). The published non-North American KFRE overestimated low risks; TH-KFRE corrected this (optimism-corrected calibration slope 0.99).

    

      Equation
      

        
LP = −0.2201 × (age/10 − 7.036) + 0.2467 × (male − 0.5642)

        
     − 0.5567 × (eGFR/5 − 7.222) + 0.4510 × (ln UACR[mg/g] − 5.137)

        
TH-KFRE 2-year risk = 1 − 0.99464 ^ exp(1.343 × LP)

        
KFRE non-North American = 1 − 0.9832 ^ exp(LP)

        
eGFR (CKD-EPI 2021) = 142 × min(Scr/κ,1)^α × max(Scr/κ,1)^−1.200 × 0.9938^age × 1.012 [female]

        
κ = 0.7 (F) / 0.9 (M); α = −0.241 (F) / −0.302 (M); Scr in mg/dL (µmol/L ÷ 88.4)

      

    

    

      Who it is for, and limits
      
Adults with CKD G3–G5 (eGFR below 60) who are not on dialysis and have not had a transplant. It gives a 2-year risk only; there is no 5-year TH-KFRE.

      
It has been validated internally in one hospital. It has not yet been tested in other Thai hospitals or in primary care. In patients aged 75 or older the study suggested it may underestimate risk. Use it to support, not replace, clinical judgement.
